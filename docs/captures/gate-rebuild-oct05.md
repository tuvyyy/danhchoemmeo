# Arched gate rebuild — 5 October 2026

The previous ironwork read as a separate illustration: a fixed decorative fanlight sat above narrow rectangular moving leaves, with little visible connection to the stone. The replacement is a pair of full-height arched leaves. Their crowns follow the photographed opening, move with their own leaf, and connect to continuous pickets and waist rails. The simpler scrollwork is welded into those members.

Fixed bolted sockets and pintles hold the moving hinge straps against the jambs. The WebGL version uses square frame stock, round bars, raised handles, hammered patina and warm sky reflections. Both renderers share `gateIronwork.ts`; the SVG fallback includes the same crowns, bars, sockets and opening pivots. Opening is capped at 94 degrees during the entrance zoom so leaves do not swing through the masonry.

Visual review: 1440 × 900 desktop, 390 × 844 mobile, closed/open and seven gate poses at 0/20/40/50/60/80/100%, in both directions. The final captures are generated locally under `docs/captures/integrated-gate/`: `before-after-contact-sheet.png`, `desktop-crown-contact-sheet.png`, and desktop/mobile swing sheets. The stone connection, crown clearance and fixed hinge positions were reviewed across the swing. Static SVG fallback was also captured.

The final iteration replaces four-sided frame tubes with extruded flat stock and bevelled edges: their broad warm faces are visible when closed, while opening reveals the dark depth of the iron. Bars meet the crown continuously, removing the dangling tips and detached ticks of the first render. The original photograph stays fully contained in the viewport. The headline remains the left-hand focal point, while simpler ironwork gives the bright garden opening breathing room. Across both opening and closing sheets, the crowns travel with the leaves and the fixed sockets stay against the stone; no separate decorative cap or empty intermediate frame interrupts the view. On mobile the photograph is intentionally smaller to retain the entire supplied composition.

Verification: TypeScript and production build; sunlit-gate interaction, close, GPU context loss/recovery, remount, no-WebGL fallback and overflow checks; restored entrance flow on desktop, mobile and mobile with reduced motion, including Escape, gallery return, theme preservation and reverse navigation.

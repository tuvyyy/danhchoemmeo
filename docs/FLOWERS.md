## Active video garden — 2026-09-27

`src/sections/FlowersSection.tsx` now plays `src/assets/Cats_nuzzling_in_night_garden_20260927145203.mp4` directly: original H.264 Full HD 1920×1080, 24 fps, 10 seconds, with no video recompression. `night-garden-poster.jpg` is a frame extracted from that same clip for loading and playback failure fallback.

Playback is muted, inline, automatic and looping, with a visible pause/play control. It pauses outside the chapter and when the document is hidden, and resumes on return unless manually paused. Autoplay remains enabled as requested even with reduced motion; the pause control is always available. Four existing messages follow progress through the first loop, then hold on the last message. The next-chapter button is available without waiting for the video download or flower animations.

`VideoGarden.css` presents one large film frame on a dark green background, with a short centered title above and the message/navigation below. Desktop preserves the full 16:9 frame. Phones use a taller 4:5 crop centered at 56% to keep both cats visible and larger; portrait tablets use 4:3. Video proportions are never stretched. No text, shading, grain, mist, duplicate moon/constellations or animated flower overlays cover the picture. A small pause control sits inside the bottom-right corner. The old flower components remain in the repository but are not mounted or preloaded by this chapter.

Verification: `corepack pnpm run typecheck`, `corepack pnpm run build`, and `node tests/garden-video.mjs` (desktop, phone and tablet; decoded resolution, autoplay, loop, pause/resume, leaving/returning to the chapter). Captures: `docs/screenshots/garden-video/`.

`GardenReel.tsx` adds an SVG metal film reel behind the right edge of the video. Its six cutouts and concentric rings rotate once every 22 seconds while the video plays. Pausing, leaving the chapter or hiding the page pauses the reel; reduced motion leaves it static. Its exposed crescent is smaller on narrow screens and does not intercept video controls. The browser suite verifies rotation, pause synchronization and screen bounds.
